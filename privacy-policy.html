Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. This policy explains, in plain terms, how Heaven Blessed Shop collects and uses information from visitors and customers.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details you submit at checkout: name, mobile number, email address, delivery address, city, state and pincode.
  • Order details: the products, quantities and order notes you submit.
  • Basic cart data stored in your browser's local storage, so your cart isn't lost if you refresh the page.

How We Use Your Information

  • To receive, review and confirm your order by email.
  • To contact you about delivery charges, order status, or any questions about your order.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

How Orders Are Processed

When you place an order, your details are sent to our order inbox using a third-party email delivery service (EmailJS). No online payment information is collected on this website, since payments are not yet processed online.

Cookies & Local Storage

This website uses your browser's local storage to remember items in your cart. No tracking cookies or advertising cookies are currently used.

Your Choices

You can clear your cart at any time from the cart page, or clear your browser's local storage to remove any saved cart data.
